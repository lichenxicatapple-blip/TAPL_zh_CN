\textbf{练习 9.2.2}

令 \(\Gamma=f:\tapltype{Bool}\to\tapltype{Bool}\)。第一棵推导树为
\[
\inferrule*[right=\rulename{T-App}]
  {\inferrule*[right=\rulename{T-Var}]
     {f:\tapltype{Bool}\to\tapltype{Bool}\in\Gamma}
     {\Gamma\vdash f:\tapltype{Bool}\to\tapltype{Bool}}
   \\
   \inferrule*[right=\rulename{T-If}]
     {\inferrule*[right=\rulename{T-False}]{ }
        {\Gamma\vdash\taplfalse:\tapltype{Bool}}
      \\
      \inferrule*[right=\rulename{T-True}]{ }
        {\Gamma\vdash\tapltrue:\tapltype{Bool}}
      \\
      \inferrule*[right=\rulename{T-False}]{ }
        {\Gamma\vdash\taplfalse:\tapltype{Bool}}}
     {\Gamma\vdash\taplif{\taplfalse}{\tapltrue}{\taplfalse}:
        \tapltype{Bool}}}
  {\Gamma\vdash
     f\,(\taplif{\taplfalse}{\tapltrue}{\taplfalse}):
     \tapltype{Bool}}
\]

第二棵树的最外层是 \rulename{T-Abs}。令
\(\Gamma'=\Gamma,x:\tapltype{Bool}\)，则其函数体的推导为
\[
\inferrule*[right=\rulename{T-App}]
  {\inferrule*[right=\rulename{T-Var}]
     {f:\tapltype{Bool}\to\tapltype{Bool}\in\Gamma'}
     {\Gamma'\vdash f:\tapltype{Bool}\to\tapltype{Bool}}
   \\
   \inferrule*[right=\rulename{T-If}]
     {\inferrule*[right=\rulename{T-Var}]
        {x:\tapltype{Bool}\in\Gamma'}
        {\Gamma'\vdash x:\tapltype{Bool}}
      \\
      \inferrule*[right=\rulename{T-False}]{ }
        {\Gamma'\vdash\taplfalse:\tapltype{Bool}}
      \\
      \inferrule*[right=\rulename{T-Var}]
        {x:\tapltype{Bool}\in\Gamma'}
        {\Gamma'\vdash x:\tapltype{Bool}}}
     {\Gamma'\vdash\taplif{x}{\taplfalse}{x}:\tapltype{Bool}}}
  {\Gamma'\vdash f\,(\taplif{x}{\taplfalse}{x}):\tapltype{Bool}}
\]
因此
\[
\inferrule*[right=\rulename{T-Abs}]
  {\Gamma'\vdash f\,(\taplif{x}{\taplfalse}{x}):\tapltype{Bool}}
  {\Gamma\vdash
    \lambda x:\tapltype{Bool}.\,f\,(\taplif{x}{\taplfalse}{x}):
    \tapltype{Bool}\to\tapltype{Bool}}
\]
